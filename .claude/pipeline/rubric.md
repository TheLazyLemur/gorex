# Difficulty rubric

Score from what the ticket says: you cannot see the code, so do not guess how many modules it touches.

1. Copy, config or a constant; the ticket gives the exact change.
2. One behaviour, stated with no open decisions.
3. Several behaviours, or a schema or data migration.
4. A cross-cutting change, a migration, or one with tricky edge cases or concurrency.
5. Design work: the ticket leaves design choices open, or says it has a high blast radius.

Confidence is how sure you are of the score, 0 to 1.
